DESIGN RULES:
- BOLD and VIBRANT. Think pop art: strong contrasts, saturated colors, no pastels.
- Cards: dark navy #232F3E with white text, OR white with black text + colored borders
- Section titles in Cherry Bomb One (large, rounded, playful)
- Use colored accent borders/tops on cards (teal, blue, yellow, purple)
- Gradient backgrounds on key slides (dark navy to slightly lighter)
- KPI numbers: LARGE, bold, in teal #00C9AF or yellow #FAC900
- Bullet points: colored circles (teal dots)
- Tables: dark header row (#232F3E, white text), alternating light/dark rows
- Icons: use emoji as visual markers (not in titles, only in body/cards)
- NO subtle shadows — use solid borders or colored outlines instead
- NO rounded soft cards — use sharp corners or minimal radius (4px)
- High contrast everywhere — this is pop art, not minimalist
